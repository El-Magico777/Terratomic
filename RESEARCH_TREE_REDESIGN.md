# Research Tree Redesign - From 22 to 12 Techs

## **🎯 Design Philosophy**

**Old Problem:** Too many invisible stat buffs (+5% attack speed, +10% casualties) that players don't notice or care about in a 40-minute IO game.

**New Solution:** Every tech unlocks something tangible you can **SEE** and **USE**. No more percentage buffs.

## **🆓 Available from Game Start (No Research Required)**

### **Units** (Previously Gated Behind Techs)

- **Submarine L1** (was gated behind Sea-2)
- **SAM L1** (was gated behind Land-3)

### **Weapons & Buildings**

- **Nuclear Weapons:** Atom Bomb, H-Bomb, MIRV (was Nuclear category)
- **Silo** (missile storage - was gated behind techs)
- **Research Labs:** (was Economy-2)
- **Basic Military Units:** Warship L1, Fighter L1, Bomber L1 (likely start units)

### **Why This Matters**

Previously 8+ techs gated basic units. Now players have immediate access to basic naval/ground defense (subs, SAMs) from turn 1, letting them focus research on **upgrades and specialization** rather than unlocking basics.

## **📈 SUMMARY**

### **What Got DROPPED:**

- 🗑️ **10 techs removed** (22 → 12)
- 🗑️ **All attack speed bonuses** (Land-1 through Land-5: +5% each = boring)
- 🗑️ **All casualty modifiers** (+10% enemy losses, -10% your losses = invisible)
- 🗑️ **All infrastructure/construction buffs** (+20% effectiveness = who notices?)
- 🗑️ **Entire Nuclear category** (nukes now free)
- 🗑️ **Research Labs unlock** (now available from start)
- 🗑️ **Scorched Earth** (road attack speed bonus nerfed, mechanic no longer necessary)

### **What Got MERGED:**

- 🔀 Sub L3 removed (Sub L2 is enough)
- 🔀 SAM L2 + SAM L3 combined into one tech
- 🔀 Fighter L2/L3 combined
- 🔀 Fighter L4 + Bomber L3 + Naval Strike combined

### **What MOVED:**

- 🔀 Doomsday Device: Nuclear-4 → Land-3
- 🔀 SAM L3: Split from Land-2, now in Land-3 with Doomsday Device

### **What's PRESERVED:**

- ✅ **All unit unlocks** (Warships, Subs, Fighters, Bombers, Paratroopers, SAM, etc.)
- ✅ **All building unlocks** (Military Academy, Roads, Hospitals, etc.)
- ✅ **Key strategic abilities** (Nuclear Subs, Naval Strike, Doomsday Device)
- ✅ **International Trade** (direct unlock, no longer a policy choice)

## **✨ BENEFITS**

1. **Faster Progression** → 12 techs means ~3-4 minutes per tech in a 40-min game (very achievable)
2. **Every Tech Matters** → No filler, every research decision is meaningful
3. **Clearer Power Spikes** → Players know exactly what they're unlocking
4. **Less Grind** → No more researching for invisible +5% bonuses
5. **Better Balance** → Nukes available early means all players can use them strategically
6. **Simpler Tree** → Easier to understand for new players
7. **Early Military Access** → Basic units and nukes from start = faster early game action

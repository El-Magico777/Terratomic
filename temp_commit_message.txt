feat: Refactor hot border colors to theme and remove darkened tiles

This commit refactors the hot border color definitions to be part of the
theme configuration, improving consistency and maintainability.

Crucially, this commit also completely removes all previously introduced
logic and state related to "darkened tiles" from the codebase.
 This includes:
- Removal of `darkenedTiles` property.
- Removal of `lastCleanupTick` property (as it was tied to darkened tile cleanup).
- Removal of the `tileUpdates.forEach` loop that populated `darkenedTiles`.
- Removal of the throttled cleanup block that handled `darkenedTiles` expiration.
- Removal of the `if (this.darkenedTiles.has(tile))` condition in `paintTerritory`.

The goal is to ensure the codebase is clean of the "darkened tiles" feature
while preserving and correctly implementing the "hot border" functionality.
